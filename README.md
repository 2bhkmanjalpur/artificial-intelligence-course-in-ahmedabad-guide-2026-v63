Understanding the Basics of Artificial Intelligence

Before diving into an AI course, it's essential to grasp the fundamental concepts of AI. This encompasses understanding what AI is, its various branches such as machine learning, deep learning, and natural language processing. A solid foundation will facilitate smoother learning in your AI course.


    Machine Learning: Algorithms that allow computers to learn from and make predictions based on data.
    Deep Learning: A subset of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers.
    Natural Language Processing (NLP): A field focused on the interaction between computers and humans through natural language.
    Computer Vision: Enabling machines to interpret and understand visual information from the world.
    Robotics: The design and use of robots to perform tasks traditionally done by humans.
    Expert Systems: AI systems that emulate the decision-making ability of a human expert.
